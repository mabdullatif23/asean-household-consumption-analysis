# ASEAN Household Consumption Analysis (2010–2024)

### Macroeconomic Determinants | Panel Data Regression | Stata 17

**Author:** Muhammad Abdul Latif  
**Institution:** Universitas Tidar, Indonesia  
**Research Year:** 2026  
**Research Type:** Undergraduate Thesis & Research Manuscript

## 1. Project Overview

This project examines the relationship between macroeconomic indicators and household consumption across five ASEAN economies: Brunei Darussalam, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, and Cambodia.

The study uses annual panel data from 2010 to 2024 and applies econometric techniques to analyze differences across countries and over time.

The project demonstrates quantitative research, data preparation, panel data regression, statistical testing, and interpretation of empirical findings.

## 2. Research Objectives

To examine the relationships between household consumption and:

1. Real Effective Exchange Rate (REER)
2. Inflation
3. Imports of Goods and Services
4. GDP per Capita

## 3. Dataset

- **Countries:** 5 ASEAN economies
- **Period:** 2010–2024
- **Observations:** 75
- **Data Structure:** Balanced Panel Data
- **Sources:** World Bank and Bruegel
- **Statistical Software:** Stata 17

## 4. Methodology

The research employs panel data regression.

The analytical procedure includes:

1. Data preparation and variable transformation
2. Descriptive statistical analysis
3. Common Effects Model (CEM)
4. Fixed Effects Model (FEM)
5. Random Effects Model (REM)
6. Panel model selection
7. Diagnostic testing
8. Final model estimation and interpretation

The Fixed Effects Model was selected as the preferred specification. Driscoll–Kraay standard errors were applied in the final estimation to address the detected heteroskedasticity.

## 5. Key Findings

The research reports the following relationships with household consumption:

| Variable | Relationship | Statistical Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Exchange Rate | Negative | Significant |
| Inflation | Negative | Significant |
| Imports of Goods and Services | Positive | Significant |
| GDP per Capita | Positive | Significant |

**Within R-squared: 0.8025**

These findings highlight the importance of macroeconomic stability, international trade conditions, and economic capacity in understanding household consumption.
